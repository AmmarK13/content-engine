How a run moves through the harness

Every stage consumes and produces a tracked artifact — nothing is passed between steps as a loose file. Each one is stored, hashed, and checkpointed before the next begins.

S00 – S50

Generate

Script, voice, avatar, sync, and captions are produced in sequence, each one a verified artifact rather than a temporary file.

S60 – G80

Verify

The assembled video passes an automated quality check, then pauses for a human reviewer to approve the exact version by hash.

G90 – S100

Publish

A disclosure record is attached confirming synthetic media, then the video is uploaded unlisted for final review.

Disclosure isn't a checkbox here

Governance is built into the pipeline itself, not bolted on after.

G90 · enforced

Every avatar-modality video is labeled as synthetic media before it's published. This isn't optional or configurable per run — the disclosure gate is a structural part of the pipeline, and a video without it does not reach the publish stage.
